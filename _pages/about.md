I'm a research scientist in the <a href='http://znamlab.org/'>lab of Petr Znamenskiy</a>.
I'm intersted in how local cortical networks perform computations in general and how
the visual system can extract the layout in the environment to form tri-dimensional
representations that can be used to guide behaviour.

This website is mostly here for me to take random notes and have easily access to them.
For research projects, see the projects tab which redirects to the relevant sections
of the lab website.
